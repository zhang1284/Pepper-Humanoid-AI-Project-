# Research session November 4 2026

## Confirmed progress

- Loaded a fresh map in our Android Localization Lab and obtained localized status on physical Pepper.
- Observed a localized short forward movement while map tracking remained active. One 30 cm target reported 24 cm map displacement; the operator estimated roughly 30 cm using tape, without a ruler.
- The operator pressed Cancel immediately after another short move started and observed the base stop. The SDK acknowledged cancellation of the move and localization. Stopping time and distance were not measured.
- Added a saved Home destination associated with the exact map fingerprint. The app built, passed Android lint, and its APK signatures verified.

## Unresolved findings

- Localization succeeded near a fresh map origin but failed in one approximately half-meter offset trial. Repeatability is not established.
- Background interaction caused physical base turns toward the operator. The rotation hold is acquired during scan preparation, leaving a window before acquisition.
- The first Home trial saved a location and completed the forward action, but return produced “GoTo failed to complete.” The operator later reported a base turn during setup; the controlled Home-return outcome is inconclusive. The turn is not proven to have caused the error.
- Whole-floor navigation, route recovery, obstacle behavior, and reliable return to the desk remain untested.
